构建产品的设计系统，包括字体、颜色、命名等
<!--more-->

## 场景
需要为产品选择合适的字体系统。

## 提示词
```
Generate a typography for my app 【Product name】according to 【Scale(e.g. Major Third)】and【Name of design guideline】. This app is 【Product descripbtion】. Ensure its readability, accessibility, and a consistent visual experience.
```

## 使用说明
> 适用于需要为产品设计字体系统的场景，确保可读性、可访问性和视觉一致性。

## 场景
需要为产品生成有吸引力的应用名称。

## 提示词
```
Act as an experienced and creative product manager. Please generate some 【memorable, catchy and beautiful】 app names indicating 【calm, lifting and supportive】 which can be described by the techniques of metaphor, simile or metonymy. The app is featured with 【an conversational AI chatbot to guide and be users' counselor that they can confide in】. The name should be within 【three words】.
```

## 使用说明
> 适用于需要为产品命名的场景。通过隐喻、比喻等修辞手法，生成有记忆点和吸引力的产品名称。
>
