撰写学术论文的文献综述部分
<!--more-->

## 场景
需要撰写学术论文的Related Work或文献综述部分。

## 提示词
```
You're a top university professor and a reviewer from 【】 journals. My goal is to provide a background of related work on 【】. My outline is 【】. 

You can refer to paper in the list and more if you want: 
1. XXX
2. XXX

You need to do the tasks below: 
1. Write a 【】 pages related work according to the writing and wording styles of above journals. 
2. Reference to academic paper
3. Provide a name list of all references
```

## 使用说明
> 适合场景：文献综述，literature review, related work

## 场景
需要撰写留学申请的个人陈述/动机信。

## 提示词
```
Role: You're a professional writer and college applicant examiner who knows well about university and colleges admissions service. You're good at storytelling and writing for Personal Statement (PS)/Letter of Motivation.

I'll provide information about myself and the university program that I intend to apply. You need to:
#1. Dive into my experience and enrich my stories based on my info, discover shine points and advantages 
#2. Identify the requirements and specialities of the uni program, summarize the applicant persona they're looking for
#3. Create an outline of my PS that matches the uni program, considering findings of #1 and #2
#4. Based on the outline of #3, write a good PS for my application at your best ability.
```

## 使用说明
> 适用于留学申请文书、个人陈述等学术材料的撰写。
> 
> **注意事项：**
> - 避免拼写和语法错误
> - 避免过度使用华丽的语言和无意义的陈词滥调
> - 不要无休止地列出课外活动
> - 不要过度使用引用或断章取义
> - 不要告诉读者他们已经知道的事情
> - 注意字数/字符限制

## 场景
需要撰写课程描述，用于留学申请。

## 提示词
```
Role: You're an admissions officer in a top university and a native English speaker. 
Task: I intend to apply {{major}}. Help me write course descriptions of my provided courses so to fit the requirement of applying major. 

A course description:
- should be no longer than 125 words
- should begin most sentences with a verb
- should be student-centered and explain how the reader would benefit from the course
- should be written in the present tense and active voice
- should not begin with "This course…"
- should not contain deliverables like "student will learn…" but instead use "students will have an opportunity to learn…"
- should not include the names of software, technology, or texts that change term to term
course descriptions should be able to carry over for multiple terms and not need frequent updating
- should not incorporate prerequisites/prerequisites, intended delivery mode, equivalencies, fees, etc., but may contain if it is repeatable and how many times
- should not include general education attributes as those will be entered elsewhere, as additional course details.
```

## 使用说明
> 适用于需要为留学申请撰写课程描述的场景。确保描述符合目标院校的要求和格式。
