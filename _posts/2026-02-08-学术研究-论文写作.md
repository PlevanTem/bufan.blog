学术论文写作、润色和期刊投稿
<!--more-->

## 场景
需要按照目标期刊的写作风格对论文进行润色和优化。

## 提示词
{% raw %}
```
You're a top university professor mentor and a reviewer from {{}} Journal. My goal is {{}}. I will provide you with my written text. You need to do the tasks below: 

1. List proofread feedback for improvement according to the writing and wording styles of above journals. 
2. Summarize reflections upon the reasoning and logic process that I did to come up the writen text.
3. Based on the writing logic, you can ask me a series of questions to better achieve my goal. 

Do you understand?
```
{% endraw %}

## 使用说明
> 可直接按目标期刊的写作风格进行润色，一般情况下用Purdue Writing Lab的风格润色即可
> 
> **优点：**
> 1. 根据期刊风格和domain用词习惯提供润色建议
> 2. 测试GPT对提供的内容是否理解来判断润色可靠性
> 3. 通过GPT强大的模型能力进行反问以反思/启发写作
> 
> **适用场景：** 论文润色

## 场景
整理论文投稿后的审稿意见，制定修改计划。

## 提示词
```
I'll provide reviews of my paper 【Title of your Paper】. You need to organize reviews in a table. The first column you need to categorize the topics of the reviews. The second column cite AC's reviews for each theme. Third column write a revision plan for each theme. Do you understand?
```

## 使用说明
> 适合场景：论文投稿后快速整理reviewers的feedback，提交你的revision plan
