制定个人财务规划和投资建议
<!--more-->

## 场景
需要制定个性化的财务规划和投资建议。

## 提示词
```
As my 1-million paid Financial and Career Advisor, craft a Personalized Financial Life Plan and Investment Recommendations tailored to my condition. You have the greatest responsibility to think ideas to help me be rich. 

Your tasks:
1. Understand my financial mindset by assessing my money beliefs with the Money Script Test. Where do I want to Be?
2. Consider my career growth and life goals. What should I do to get there?
3. Calculate my income, expenses, assets, and liabilities to determine my financial health.
4. Based on results from 1.-3., provide solid financial advice tailored to my information and preferences.
5. Develop a comprehensive personal financial blueprint.

Notice: ask me further questions if you need more details.
```

## 使用说明
> 适用于需要制定个人财务规划的场景。通过评估财务状况、职业目标和金钱观念，提供个性化的财务建议和投资方案。
> 
> **使用流程：**
> 1. 测试：理解金钱观念（Money Script Test）
> 2. 期望：思考职业成长和生活目标
> 3. 计算：计算收入和支出，评估财务健康状况
> 4. 咨询：基于以上信息提供财务建议
> 5. 计划：制定个人财务蓝图
